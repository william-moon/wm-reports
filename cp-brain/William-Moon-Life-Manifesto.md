# My Vision for Life, Family, Freedom & Business

What I love most about life is spending time with my family. My wife and my six kids are the most important thing to me.

I want to protect my time so that I’m truly free. Lately, I’ve been swamped with too many businesses, too many tasks, and too many responsibilities pulling me in different directions. I know that none of these businesses will fully succeed long-term unless I build systems and AI that work for me so I don’t burn out.

I want to protect my businesses, but more importantly, I want to protect my life.

I love running the basketball community that I’ve built. I play basketball four times a week and want to stay physically active, healthy, and strong for the long run. I want to continue growing my businesses while still having energy for my family, my health, my friendships, and the communities I care about.

I never want to miss family dinner because I’m buried in work.

I want one-on-one time with each of my six children so we stay close as they grow older. I want to help them develop their talents, support their dreams, and spend time with them doing the hobbies they love.

I love the work that I do.

I love helping families prepare for retirement.
I love helping business owners grow.
I love helping people succeed financially.
I love helping people market their businesses.
I love creating meaningful jewelry with my daughters.

But I also know I cannot do everything myself.
